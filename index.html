BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

Questions,
answered.

Every order is different. Here are a few helpful starting points.

Have another question? ↗
What products does Wonderxasia focus on?

Our showcase focuses on Indian spices, blended masalas and shelf-stable pantry products such as rice, pulses, beans, dry fruits, nuts and selected packaged goods. The live range can be discussed against your market and requirement.

Do you handle bulk export enquiries?

Yes. Share your estimated volume, product form and destination so the appropriate minimum order and supply options can be discussed. Minimums vary by product, pack and sourcing arrangement.

Can I request a particular grade or packing format?

Include your preferred grade, processing or pack details in the enquiry. Available formats and customization are confirmed for each product and order.

Can samples or product reports be discussed?

Where available, sample options and supporting product information can be discussed during sourcing. Analytical reports and related documents are product- and shipment-specific.

Which export documents can be coordinated?

Documentation is reviewed against product and destination rules. The catalogue lists FSSAI, origin, analytical, health and phytosanitary documents as available or applicable. Requirements and availability should be confirmed before order commitment.

How are shipping and delivery arranged?

Freight and shipment arrangements can be discussed against destination, volume and agreed terms. Feasibility, timelines and responsibilities are confirmed for each shipment.

Does the enquiry form place an order?

No. The form is a frontend-only enquiry builder. It does not transmit your details or place an order. It prepares text that you can copy and share through your chosen contact channel.
